August 2026 Tournament
Rules
The basics
4s co-ed. At least one guy and one girl on the sand at all times
Six on a roster, four on the court. Sub between games, not mid game
Call your own lines. If nobody agrees, replay the point
Be at your net ten minutes before your first game. The schedule does not wait
Short a player? Grab a sub from another team and tell the net captain
Scoring
Rally scoring. Every serve is a point for somebody
Pool games go to 21, win by 2
The championship game has no cap. Win by 2, however long it takes
Switch sides at 11. Losing team serves first the next game
Report the score in the app the moment the game ends. The bracket runs off it
How the day runs
1Pool play. Every team plays every other team in its pool once
2Seeding. Wins first, then point differential, then head to head
3Double elimination bracket. Lose twice and you are done
4Championship. No cap, win by 2, everybody watches
Being a good human

Play hard and keep it clean. This is rec sand volleyball at Woodmen Valley Park and everybody has work on Monday.

No cleats, no glass, no dogs off leash. Pack out what you pack in. The park stays open to us because we leave it better than we found it.

Weather calls go out in the app and the group chat by 8 AM. If it is lightning we sit for thirty minutes, then reassess.
