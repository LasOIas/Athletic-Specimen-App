Motion

Every state in the app swaps instantly today: a score lands with no acknowledgement, a screen appears with no origin, a number changes and nobody sees it. This is the system that fixes it — five durations, four curves, and every action in the app expressed in them. Transform and opacity only, micro-moves under 10px, nothing past 200ms except the one celebration a tournament earns.

Now shipped. Every screen in the app loads this system: the screen arrives, lists land in sequence, buttons and rows answer a press, sheets and menus grow from what opened them, changed values bump, and the championship bar is the one place allowed to celebrate. Nothing here is per-screen — it hangs off the patterns the screens already have, so the next screen inherits it for free.

If only six ship
  1. Button press — every tap in the app, one line of CSS, the biggest felt difference.
  2. One-tap fix → done, and undo — the Manage hub already works this way; the motion is what makes it trustworthy.
  3. Screen push and back — kills the "where am I" that costs organizers time mid-game.

  5. Rows arrive / leave — pools and teams change constantly and currently teleport.
  6. Toast — one confirmation pattern, so nothing else has to interrupt.
