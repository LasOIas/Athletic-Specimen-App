Moving players — drag

The direction you picked, built out. Every name carries a grip; drag it onto another team and the player you land on trades places. Team totals update live while you hover, so the cost of the trade is visible before you let go — and the two rails hold: Undo after every drop, and a warning when the swap leaves teams apart on skill.

1Resting

Grips sit at the left of every name, one line of instruction, nothing else added. Skill stays on the right so the roster still reads as a list at a glance.

2Mid-drag

The name lifts and follows the finger, its old spot holds open as a dashed slot, and the team under it highlights. Both totals preview the trade (−6.5 / +6.5) and the row names who you'd swap with.

3Dropped

Rosters and totals settle, an Undo strip sits under the list, and because this trade left Team 2 and Team 3 11.0 apart, the drift warning fires with the fix suggested.
